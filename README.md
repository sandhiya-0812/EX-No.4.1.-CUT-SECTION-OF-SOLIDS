# EX No.4.1. CUT SECTION OF SOLIDS
## DATE:26/10/2026
## Name:SANDHIYA. G
## Reg.No.:26009640

## AIM
To create the cut section of a given solid model using CAD software by applying suitable sectioning techniques to understand the internal features, shape, and geometry of the component.

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3.16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE:
1. First, switch on the computer and open the required CAD software, such as Fusion 360, SolidWorks, or AutoCAD. Allow the software to load completely and enter the modelling workspace.

2. Select the New Design option to create a new file. Set the required units, preferably millimetres, and ensure that the necessary sketching, solid modelling, and section view tools are available.
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825274-c6a8d3ee-fb19-4641-b915-8d2e000c40a8.png)

3. Study the given solid model or engineering drawing carefully. Observe its external shape, dimensions, geometrical features, and the arrangement of its different portions. Identify the internal features such as holes, cavities, slots, hollow regions, and other details that may not be visible from the exterior.

4. Create the required solid model using suitable sketching and modelling tools if the component is not already available. Draw the basic profile, apply the required dimensions and constraints, and use operations such as Extrude, Revolve, Cut, or other solid modelling commands to form the complete component.
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825282-0d3de4b8-b6d9-446a-ae2a-461955057399.png)

5. Once the solid model is completed, select the appropriate sectioning or cutting plane based on the required sectional view. Choose a suitable plane, such as the XY, YZ, or XZ plane, or create a new reference plane passing through the required portion of the solid.


6. Activate the Section Analysis or Section View command from the CAD toolbar. Select the solid body to be sectioned and specify the cutting plane. Adjust the position and orientation of the cutting plane so that it passes through the required region of the component.
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825301-194365b4-69d2-42ad-8545-a68d3ca1f442.png)

7. Move the cutting plane along the required direction and observe how the solid is divided. Adjust the section depth and position to expose the internal features clearly. Ensure that the cutting plane passes through the important regions of the component, such as holes, cavities, slots, and internal surfaces.


8. Observe the resulting cut section carefully and examine the internal geometry exposed by the cutting operation. Identify the shape, size, position, and arrangement of the internal features. Compare the sectional view with the original solid model to understand the relationship between the external and internal surfaces.
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825314-b90f6aba-da26-4f0d-8d61-a03e8ad0d765.png)

9. Adjust the viewing direction and use the zoom, pan, and orbit tools to inspect the sectioned component from different angles. Observe the sectional surfaces and check whether the internal features are clearly visible and properly represented.

10. Make the necessary adjustments to the cutting plane position, orientation, or depth to obtain the required sectional view. Ensure that the section accurately represents the internal geometry of the component and that the important features are not hidden.
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825328-205fac21-1322-4d67-894b-6fd92e454e26.png)

11. If required, modify the display settings of the sectional view by enabling suitable section shading, hatch patterns, or edge display options. Adjust the visual representation to clearly distinguish the cut surfaces from the remaining solid portions.

12. Finally, save the completed sectional model or section view using a suitable file name and format. Ensure that the sectional view is properly displayed and saved for future reference, editing, and documentation.
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198825337-455049de-f053-48b8-9dd0-ebeb189c0dde.png)
